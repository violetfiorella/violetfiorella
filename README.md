# Hi, I'm Violet!

I am currently a Data Science and Music Industry student at Northeastern University's Khoury College, and will be **graduating in December 2026**. My work so far has primarily focused on applying data science to the music business, and I've gained experience at several of the industry's leading organizations.

At **WaterTower Music**, Warner Bros. Discovery's music label, I supported soundtrack campaigns for some of the studio's biggest releases, including *Superman* and *Weapons*. After identifying inefficiencies in the label's manual metadata workflow, I proposed and built a program that automated metadata loading into its backend systems, cutting processing time by over 99% and reducing an estimated **six months of manual work** to just **two hours**. At **Arista Records** of Sony Music Entertainment, I built a scraper tool that pulled data from the Spotify Web API and other streaming and social platforms to identify unsigned artists showing early breakout potential, supporting the label's talent discovery efforts. My code discovered multiple then-unknown artists that have since secured high-value record deals and **top 5 spots on the Billboard Hot 100**. I've also supported business intelligence reporting at **Warner Chappell Music**, contributed to royalty prediction models at **SoundExchange**, and scouted emerging talent for **Columbia Records**. Outside of music, I've developed machine learning solutions for financial data at **Brown Brothers Harriman**.

While most of my career so far has been in the music industry, music is an exceptionally data-heavy business. Every stream, playlist add, and royalty payment generates data across dozens of platforms and territories, and working with it has prepared me for analytics in any field with large, messy, fast-moving data. I'm seeking data science and analytics roles in any industry where data drives decisions. I am currently based in Los Angeles, but am open to relocation as well as any remote opportunities.
